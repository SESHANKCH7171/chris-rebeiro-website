## 3. Signature Interactive Artifact: "The Starlight Constellation" Canvas

> **Why NOT a rotating 3D cube:** Rotating cubes feel templated and undermine executive authority.  
> **What to build instead:** A serene, razor-sharp HTML5 `<canvas>` backdrop inside the dark Hero (`#0B1020`) called **"The Starlight Signal Constellation"**.

### Technical Specification
- Render ~45 crisp, low-opacity star/data nodes drifting slowly across the right and upper portions of the dark Hero (`rgba(212, 163, 81, 0.45)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`).
- Connect nearby nodes with hairline constellation lines (`rgba(212, 163, 81, 0.14)`) that subtly brighten and crystallize into structured geometric triangles when the user's cursor glides near them — a quiet, poetic visual metaphor for **Starlight Analytics** turning scattered consumer data into a clear strategic signal.
- Keep it calm, minimal, 60fps via `requestAnimationFrame`, zero heavy WebGL/Three.js dependencies, and automatically static under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
